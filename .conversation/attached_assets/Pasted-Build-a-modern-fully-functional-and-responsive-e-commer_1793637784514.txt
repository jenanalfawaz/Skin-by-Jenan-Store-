Build a modern, fully functional, and responsive e-commerce web application for a skincare brand called "Skin by Jenan".

1. Brand Identity & Styling:
- Store Name: Skin by Jenan
- Mission: Selling 100% natural, safe skincare products, specializing in face masks and eye patches.
- Color Palette: Dark pink (primary accent), light pink (secondary background/accents), and crisp white (clean base).
- Typography: Clear, highly readable, elegant, and modern sans-serif font.
- Slogan: "A little mask, A lot of glow" (prominently displayed in the Hero Section).

2. Navigation Bar Layout:
- Main Links (Left/Center): Home | Shop | Best Sellers | Find Your Mask
- Utility Actions (Right): Search | Account | Wishlist | Shopping Bag (Cart)

3. Page Structure & Key Features:
- Home Page:
  * Hero Section: Eye-catching banner featuring the slogan, brand background image/video mockup, and a primary CTA button ("Shop Now").
  * Offers & Promotions section.
  * Best Sellers highlight section.
  * Ingredients Spotlight section: Clear cards explaining the natural ingredients used and their skin benefits.
  * Skin Tips section: Short, helpful skincare tips.
- Shop Page: Displays all face masks and eye patches with category filters and sort options.
- Best Sellers Page: Filtered view of top-rated and trending products.
- Find Your Mask: Interactive mini-quiz page that asks users a few simple questions (e.g., skin type, main goal) and recommends the perfect mask product.
- Contact Section: Simple contact form and details (in footer or dedicated section).

4. Product Cards & UI Requirements:
- Each product card must include: Image, Product Title, Price, Star Rating, "Add to Cart" button, and "Add to Wishlist" heart icon.

5. Interactive UX & Micro-interactions (Required):
- Hover Effects: Smooth transition on buttons, nav items, and interactive product cards (zoom or elevate effect).
- Click Actions: 
  * Clicking "Add to Cart" opens a side-drawer or updates the Shopping Bag counter with a notification toast.
  * Clicking the heart icon toggles Favorite/Wishlist state.
- Dynamic Search: A functional search bar that filters products instantly as the user types.
